RULANTICA · VILDFÅL · A PERSONAL ACCOUNT

A water slide. A broken front tooth.

On 21 February 2024, a visit to Rulantica ended with part of my front tooth broken after riding Vildfål. I say I followed the safety instructions. The park's insurer later rejected liability and maintained that decision after review.

21.02.2024Date of incident VildfålSlide concerned DeniedLiability claim
01WHAT HAPPENED

I followed the instructions. I still left injured.

During my visit, I rode Vildfål, Rulantica's free-fall slide. According to my account, I used the attraction as instructed. During the descent, part of a front tooth broke off.

I reported the incident and sought compensation. The correspondence that followed acknowledges my report of the broken tooth, but rejects the conclusion that Rulantica was at fault. I continued asking for the incident and the safety of the attraction to be taken seriously.

04OFFICIAL SAFETY CONTEXT

“At your own risk” should not end the conversation.

Rulantica's current official information lists Vildfål for visitors aged 12 and over and at least 140 cm tall. Its published rules require visitors to follow the attraction-specific instructions and state that slides are used at the visitor's own risk. Those rules explain the insurer's position—but in my view, they should not prevent a transparent investigation when an injury is reported despite compliance.
